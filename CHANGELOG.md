# Changes

Each release is listed here, newest first. The Settings page shows the notes for a new version when you check for updates, so keep each entry short and in plain words.

## 1.5.1

- The numbers at the top sit in one panel, with a bar showing how full your disk is.
- Labels such as Safe to remove, Worth a look and Used with Claude are now soft coloured pills, with no dot.
- The summary cards line up at the top, so their numbers sit level.
- Lighter grey panels, slightly smaller section headings and less space between the numbers and the cards.

## 1.5.0

- A file type menu next to the project menu. It groups what the scan found (Documents, Images, Video, Audio, Design files, 3D, Archives, Code and more), and each group opens to show its file types, such as MOV or PDF. Only types that were found are listed.
- Settings has a new section, The list, where you can turn off thumbnails, folder paths and descriptions for a shorter list. Hovering over a name still shows its folder and description.
- The list shows the biggest 50 items first, with a Show more button for the rest. The tabs and filters stay at the top as you scroll.
- Where the space goes shows your top 5 projects, with a link to show them all.
- The results page starts with the numbers: the big heading, description and shapes are gone, and the scan date stays.

## 1.4.2

- The shapes on the scanning screen are the same size as on the results page.

## 1.4.1

- In the bottom bar, Clear selection sits to the left of Select all safe and only shows once something is ticked.

## 1.4.0

- A folder of frames has a See all files button. It opens every frame in the folder, so you can tick and remove single ones instead of the whole folder. Frames you move to the Trash leave the panel, and the folder's size and count go down.
- A project menu next to the search box lets you show or hide whole projects. Clicking a project in the chart shows only that one.
- Tidier layout: the checkbox lines up with each name, the summary cards and shapes take less room, sections are evenly spaced, and the note at the bottom no longer sits under the bar.

## 1.3.1

- New pictures on the GitHub page showing the thumbnails and the video preview.

## 1.3.0

- Images and videos have a thumbnail in the list. Click it for a bigger view, so you can see what a file is without opening its folder.
- Videos play right in the page. If your browser can't play one (ProRes in Chrome, for example), Open in video player opens it in your usual player.
- A folder of frames shows its first frame.
- Update now in the update box runs the update for you and shows what it's doing. When it's done, Restart starts the new version and reloads the page.

## 1.2.4

- The update steps include refreshing the list of versions first, so the Terminal command always installs the newest one.
- If you skip versions, the update box lists what's new in each of them, not only the latest.

## 1.2.3

- Check for updates sees a new version within a minute of its release. Before, it could say you were up to date for up to 5 minutes afterwards.

## 1.2.2

- The three summary cards all use white text.
- On the Settings page, the button at the top right takes you back to your results, so you can switch between the two.

## 1.2.1

- While it scans, the progress and the Stop button sit in a bar at the bottom, so the page no longer jumps about with long file names.
- The shapes show a different number each time, from a few to most of the grid.

## 1.2.0

- Nothing is scanned until you press Start: the page always opens with your folders ticked so you can check them first.
- A Stop button while it scans takes you back to your folders.
- The shapes shuffle by themselves while it scans.

## 1.1.0

- The Creative Mash shapes are back beside the heading. You get a new random set each time, and clicking them shuffles them.
- New screenshots and a Settings section in the README.

## 1.0.1

- Claude now recognises everyday requests like "my Mac is running out of space" or "what can I delete?".

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Finds build folders, backups, copies, duplicates, unused media and caches across your project folders.
- Asks which folders to look at on first use, with suggestions from your Claude Code projects.
- Choose folders with your computer's own folder window, or paste a location.
- Remove folders you don't want listed. Removed suggestions stay hidden until you add them again.
- Settings page with your folders and a check for updates.
- Works on macOS, Windows and Linux. Everything removed goes to the Trash or Recycle Bin first.
